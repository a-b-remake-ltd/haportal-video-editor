# Kinetic headlines — the key sentence builds on screen, word by word

The signature of premium talking-head reels (agency interview looks like the Rollin reference): on the
punchiest sentence of a beat, a 1-3 line headline builds **in sync with the voice**. Each
word lands as it is said, framing words thin and white, the keyword bold in the brand colour.
It holds until the sentence ends and then **hard-cuts**. No slide, no bounce, no exit.

Generated by `scripts/kinetic.py` from `media.json "headlines"`. `build_index.py` emits it
and hides the captions over its window. Never hand-place one in `index.html`.

---

## When to use one

- **The key sentence of a beat.** The claim, the turn, the line you would quote. Not
  setup, not a list, not the CTA.
- **5-7 per reel** (a ~45-60 s monologue). Fewer reads thin; on every sentence they stop
  meaning anything (the build warns above 8).
- **The window ends where the headline's words end.** Captions are hidden under it, so any
  other word spoken inside the window is never seen (the build names it and says where to end).
- **Never more than one on screen.** Overlapping windows stop the build.
- **On the speaker, not on a designed moment.** A headline sits over the talking head (the
  chest). Over a full-frame graphic the graphic itself carries the words.
- **Short.** 2-6 words, 1-3 lines. A whole sentence of 10 words is a caption, not a headline.
- With a reference whose headlines build word by word, `style.kinetic` is on
  (`apply_style.py`) and the build reminds you when `media.json` has none.

## The words: verbatim, always

The headline is the speaker's **exact words**, in order: a contiguous stretch of the
sentence, or a subset of it with fillers left out. Emphasis comes from weight and colour,
never from rewording. "פרסונל ברנד" stays "פרסונל ברנד", never "מיתוג אישי". Every word is
matched against `src/words.json`. A word that is not found is spread evenly between its
neighbours and **warned**. That warning almost always means the text is not verbatim. Fix
the text, do not silence it.

## media.json

```json
"headlines": [
  {"id": "h1", "text": "הטעות הזאת / *עולה לכם כסף.*"},
  {"id": "h2", "text": "את *הכלי* / ^החדש^ שלנו,", "start": 25.84, "style": "classic"},
  {"id": "h3", "text": "זה ללא / ^הרשמה^ / *נגיש* *לכולם*"}
]
```

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | `h1`, `h2`, ... | unique element id |
| `text` | (required, or `lines`) | the markup below |
| `lines` | | list form: `[[["לא","thin"],["במהות","thin"]], [["פרסונל","partner"],["ברנד","key"]]]`. Role per word, `"thin sm"` marks a small line, an optional 3rd element is that word's land time |
| `start`, `end` | found in the transcript | the window. Without `start` the phrase is searched in the whole transcript (said twice → warned, give `start`). Without `end` it holds to the end of the sentence (below) |
| `style` | `style.kinetic` if it names one, else `rollin` | `rollin`, `ko` or `bold` (below) |
| `on` | `footage` | `paper` = ink + `--hl-on-light`, no shadow, no scrim, for a light surface underneath |
| `place` | `chest` (`upper` with `behind`) | `chest`, `upper`, `center`; or `top: px` |
| `behind` | `false` | `true` = the words sit BETWEEN the wall and the person (below) |
| `matte` | `assets/matte_<id>.webm` | the person matte `behind` needs |
| `size` | per style | nominal px of a full line at 1080 wide; the fit only ever shrinks it |
| `font` | `brand` | `display` = `var(--display-font)`. Only if that face has a thin AND a bold weight |
| `hide_captions` | `true` | `false` keeps the captions on, and the stack is lifted above the caption plate |
| `scrim` | `auto` | soft dark radial scrim behind the type when the footage there is bright |
| `times` | | per-word land times, overriding the transcript. Last resort |

### The markup

| Mark | Role | Look |
|---|---|---|
| ` / ` | line break | a slash between two digits (`24/7`) stays text |
| `*w*` | keyword | `var(--hl-on-dark)`, 800 |
| `^w^` | partner | a lighter tint of the keyword colour, 500 |
| `+w+` | bold | white, 800 |
| `_w_` | thin | white, 200 |
| `=w=` | gradient | the closer or the emotional word: a gradient (electric blue → lavender → pink, from the brand tokens), 800 |
| `~w~` | small line | the whole line at 0.6×, for a lead-in ("הרבה שואלים:") |
| plain | the style's default | thin for `rollin` / `ko`, bold for `bold` |

Markers may span words (`*פרסונל ברנד*`). A lone trailing marker (`ברנד*`) marks one word.
Bold is `+`, not `!`, because a Hebrew sentence may end on "!". Check a line with
`python3 scripts/kinetic.py parse "<markup>"`.

The same markers work in the kit's word stacks (`kit.stack`, the hook opener `intro=`).
Headlines stay right-aligned at right 160 on the chest (a design choice): they are the one
exception to the centring rule of `references/grid.md`.

One keyword per headline, two at most (a partner + keyword pair counts as one idea).
Colour everything and nothing stands out.

## Timing (automatic)

- **Each word lands as it is spoken.** Land time = spoken start − `cutting.lead` (0.06 s),
  snapped to a frame, so the word has resolved by the time the sound arrives.
- **Start**: the first word's land time, but never back across the cut it sits behind. A
  headline that opens a segment starts on the boundary, like a caption card.
- **End**: holds to the end of the sentence. It cuts on the segment boundary in the pause
  when there is one (the shot change), else exactly where the next caption card starts
  (next word − lead), at most 1.2 s after the last word. That frame is where the captions
  come back.
- The build prints the word → time table. `python3 scripts/kinetic.py plan` prints it with
  the spoken word each one matched.

## The three looks

| Style | Entrance (per word) | Line height | Default role | From |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rollin` | faint grey → its colour, 0.20 s, no movement | 1.02 | thin | Rollin STYLE.md §4 |
| `ko` | opacity + 14 px rise + blur 6 → rest, 0.24 s | 1.18 | thin | KO `kit.py word()` |
| `bold` | as rollin, 0.18 s | 1.04 | bold | Rollin "בא לי / לעקוב / אחריו" |

Entrances start from opacity 0, not the reference's 0.15. A `fromTo` rewound past its start
renders its FROM state, and a backward seek must show nothing, not a ghost. On the first
frame the word is already ~30 % in, which reads as the same faint entry. Exit is a hard
`tl.set(opacity 0)` at `end`.

## Placement (the grid)

- **Right-aligned RTL stack hanging from x 920** (`1080 − headline_right_margin`), inside
  the safe zone x 60-940 and clear of the action rail.
- **Size is measured, not guessed.** Every line is measured in headless Chrome in the real
  face and weights, and the font shrinks until the widest line fits the 860 px between x 60
  and x 920. Under ~64 px the build tells you to break the line instead.
- **`chest` hangs under the measured chin.** KO's top 980 assumed wide framing; on a close
  selfie the chin sits lower. Run `python3 scripts/framing_map.py assets/aroll.mp4` first:
  with `build/framing.json` present, the stack's top = the chin + 36 px. The chin used is
  the LOWEST credible one: the p80 of the per-frame chins (the head nods) and the face
  centre + 1.0 face widths (a beard hides the jaw from the skin mask; on two real bearded
  takes the measured chin sat 50-100 px above the beard's bottom), capped at the collar
  when the chest band was found. Every number is sanity-checked first: a chin misread in a
  frame (a hand or a microphone read as the collar), an upside-down chest band or an
  implausible face is named and ignored, and a `framing.json` older than the A-roll is
  flagged. Without the file the face is measured on the A-roll inside the window (the
  outro's skin mask), and a misread there (a landscape 4K source) falls back to y 980 with a
  warning. `kinetic.py plan` prints which source placed each headline.
- **The punch-in moves the chin.** The A-roll scales about 50% 30% (beat scale 1.02 times
  any `punch` step). The chin is mapped through the LARGEST scale active in the headline's
  window, so a 1.14 punch (≈ +75 px at the chin) cannot drop the beard onto a stack placed
  for scale 1. If the stack then has no room under the chin it goes above the head when
  that is clear, else the build warns.
- **The build warns whenever the stack still overlaps the face box** (head top to chin,
  face centre ± 0.62 face widths, on screen, at the window's scale). Fix it with fewer
  lines, a smaller `size`, `place: upper`, or `top`.
- **Never over the caption band unless the captions are hidden.** With `hide_captions: false`
  the stack is lifted above the caption plate, and the build warns if that puts it on the face.
- **Readability is measured.** The reference look is thin white type with only a soft
  shadow, which disappears on a white shirt or a bright wall. The A-roll behind the stack is
  sampled, and when it is bright (75th-percentile luma > 0.52) a soft dark radial scrim
  goes behind the type: no box, no edge. Set `"scrim": false` to refuse it, `true` to force it.
- `grid.py check` covers every word. Tight leading makes the font boxes overlap (the glyphs
  do not), so rollin/bold words carry `data-layout-allow-overlap` for `hyperframes check`.

## Behind the speaker (`"behind": true`)

The Rollin look outside the hook: big words between the wall and the person, the head
passing in front of them. The headline goes under the speaker's matte of exactly its
window: `#aroll` (z 20) < the words (z 24) < the matte video (z 26, same box and
`transform-origin: 50% 30%` as the A-roll, and every beat / punch scale mirrored by
`tl.set`). Videos end inclusive and divs exclusive, so the matte runs `end − 1 frame`, as
in the hook.

```json
{"id": "h4", "text": "בא לי / *לעקוב* / אחריו", "style": "bold", "size": "huge", "behind": true}
```

It needs a person matte of the window. When it is missing the build stops and prints the
exact commands, for example:

```bash
mkdir -p build && ffmpeg -v error -y -ss 30.000 -i assets/aroll.mp4 -t 2.400 -an \
  -vf scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920 \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 12 -pix_fmt yuv420p build/span_h4.mp4
python3 scripts/matte.py build/span_h4.mp4 assets/matte_h4.webm    # torch, ~30 s per 2.4 s on Apple silicon
```

The build also stops when the matte is not the composition's size, when its length is off
the window by more than 2 frames (the window moved: a re-cut, a new start/end), when it is
older than the A-roll, when the window is inside the hook (the hook has its own matte),
on a panel beat, or while B-roll or a designed moment covers the A-roll (the matte would
paint the speaker over them). Each message names the fix. Re-matte after every re-cut.

Placement: the default is `upper`. The point is words passing BEHIND THE HEAD, not text
buried under the torso, so the build measures how much of the stack the matte covers at
mid-window and warns above 45 %. Then move it (`top`, fewer or shorter lines). LOOK at a
`hyperframes snapshot` across the window: the keyword must still read.

## Captions while a headline is up

The headline says the sentence, so the caption would say it twice. Each headline's window
goes into `build/caption_hide.json` (merged with any element marked `"hide_captions": true`
and `media.json "hide_captions": [[a, b], ...]`). `caption_layer.py` makes the layer
transparent there, splitting a card that straddles an edge. `captions.json` is never touched
and the layer keeps its exact length, so preflight continuity and `finish.py` are
unaffected. Inline captions get hard `tl.set` hides instead.

## Hebrew

- `direction: rtl`, right-aligned. The first word lands on the RIGHT. Check it on a frame.
- Latin is isolated in `<bdi class="ltr">`, and `AI`-like acronyms get `class="ltr ai"`
  (Roboto Slab: in heavy Hebrew faces a capital I has no serif and "AI" reads "Al"). Write
  the word as spoken, prefix included: `ה-AI`, not `AI`. The transcriber's split "ה" +
  "-AI" is merged before matching.
- **A number and its unit stay on one line**: `20 אלף`, never `20 / אלף`. The build warns.
- Keep the speaker's punctuation (`כסף.`): it is verbatim, and in RTL the period sits on
  the left, where it belongs.

## Worked example: Rollin, "לא פרסונל ברנד במהות שלהם"

The reference (≈11.8 s) builds over the chest:

```
              לא          ← thin white, the framing word
     פרסונל ברנד          ← "פרסונל" light-blue bold, "ברנד" strong blue bold
     במהות שלהם           ← thin
```

In this skill:

```json
{"id": "h2", "text": "לא / ^פרסונל^ *ברנד* / במהות שלהם", "style": "rollin"}
```

Each word is found in `src/words.json` and lands as said. The stack hangs from x 920 under
the chin, the keyword takes `--hl-on-dark` (the client's brand blue, not Rollin's #2F9BFF),
the partner a lighter tint of it, the captions disappear for exactly those frames, and the
whole block cuts out when the next sentence starts. The reference sets "שלהם" in a pale tint
at a thin weight. Here it stays thin white: there is no thin-partner role. Note it in
STYLE.md as a deliberate simplification.

## Verify on the render

Pull frames every 0.1 s across each headline (`ffmpeg -vf fps=10,tile=6x4`) and look:
words land as spoken, RTL order right, keyword in the brand colour, nothing on the face,
captions gone for the window and back on the next frame, nothing outside the safe zone.
